Privacy Policy — توبتى (Tawabti)

Last updated: 2026-10-16

توبتى ("Tawabti", "the App") is a prayer times and dhikr companion application. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version distributed on Google Play.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Location Data

The App uses your device's precise or approximate location to calculate prayer times and determine Qibla direction.

  • Location is requested only after you grant permission in a system dialog.
  • Location is used only while the app is in use. The App does not have (or need) background location access, and it does not schedule prayer-time notifications or alarms. Your last known location is saved on your device only so prayer times can be shown.
  • Coordinates are processed on your device to compute prayer times.
  • To display a human-readable city name, the App may send approximate coordinates to the operating system's geocoding service.
  • We do not store your location on any server.

1.2 Notifications & Messaging

With your permission, the App shows local notifications (planner and wird reminders, a daily Islamic tip and an evening tasbih summary) and receives messages via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). FCM assigns your device a registration token to deliver messages. Messages may include app updates, announcements, and Ramadan greetings.

1.3 App Settings & Usage Data

The App uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust update announcements and feature configuration. Firebase may automatically collect device and app information (such as device model, OS version, and app version) associated with a resettable installation identifier.

1.4 Data Stored On Your Device

Your tasbih counts, wird plan and progress, voluntary prayer and fasting records, make-up (qada) prayer balances, appointments and reminders (titles, times, notes, meeting links), favorite library items and the media links you attach, chosen settings, calculation method, and app icon preference are stored locally on your device (local database / preferences). This data stays on your device unless you turn on the optional sync described in 1.6. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

1.5 Calendar (optional)

If you choose to link a device calendar, the App asks for calendar permission, then reads events from the calendar you select so you can import them, and writes an event to it only when you export one of your appointments. Calendar data is used on your device and is not sent to us. Calendars from other apps and accounts (for example Google Calendar) appear only if they are already synchronized on your device; the App does not sign in to any Google account and does not create Google Meet meetings. A Meet link you save is just text that opens in your browser or the Meet app.

1.6 Optional sync to your own server

Sync is off by default. Nothing is uploaded until you enter a server address (HTTPS only) and an access token under My local data → Data sync. Once enabled, whenever the device is online the App uploads your saved records (appointments and reminders, worship and qada records, library favorites and attached links) to that server only. We do not operate that server, do not receive this data, and cannot see or delete it; whoever runs the server is responsible for it. The access token is stored on your device. Turning sync off stops further uploads and removes the saved address and token, but copies already uploaded remain on that server until its operator deletes them.

1.7 Reading helper over other apps (optional)

If you turn on the floating reading helper, the App uses Android's "Display over other apps" permission to show a small card (today's wird pages, Done and Close) on top of the reading app you selected. A foreground-service notification is shown while it is visible. Separately and optionally, you may grant Usage access so the card can hide itself when you leave that app; the App then checks, on your device, only whether your selected reading app is in the foreground. It does not record, store, or transmit which apps you use. Both permissions are off until you enable them, and the wird works without them. The App also lists installed Quran reading apps on your device so you can pick one; that list is not uploaded. Opening a reading app never marks anything as read; you confirm your reading yourself.

1.8 Media you attach

Audio, video, and image links you add to library items are loaded directly from the websites that host them. Those hosts can see your IP address and request details, as with any web content. Direct video files play inside the App; links to video sites such as YouTube open in your browser or their own app. The App collects nothing about what you watch or listen to.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — Cloud Messaging and Remote Config. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Shorebird — delivers small Dart code patches (performance fixes, bug fixes) to the App over an encrypted connection. Shorebird does not receive your personal data. See https://shorebird.dev/privacy
  • Geocoding services — approximate coordinates may be converted to a city name.
  • Your sync server — only if you enable sync in 1.6; it is chosen and operated by you or someone you trust, not by us.
  • Media hosts — the websites behind links you attach in the library (1.8).

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no accounts and no chat. Notes and links you add stay on your device or on the server you configure.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • You may deny or revoke any permission at any time in your device's Settings.
  • You may uninstall the App at any time; all locally stored data is deleted.
  • "Restore default settings" in the App restores prayer, alert and appearance settings; your worship records and appointments are kept. To erase them, clear the App's data in Android settings or uninstall.
  • You may revoke the calendar, overlay and usage-access permissions, or turn sync off, at any time.
  • We hold no personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. Data uploaded to a server you configured must be deleted with that server's operator. For questions, contact us below.

6. Permissions the App Requests

  • Location (while the app is in use) — prayer times & Qibla.
  • Notifications — planner and wird reminders, the daily tip and the tasbih summary.
  • Exact alarms — so the personal alarms and appointment reminders you set ring at the precise time even in Doze mode.
  • Full-screen intent — shows a personal alarm you scheduled over the lock screen when it rings.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — keeps the alarm sound you scheduled playing reliably.
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback for tasbih.
  • Calendar (read / write) — optional; import and export your appointments with a calendar you choose.
  • Display over other apps — optional; the floating reading helper (1.7).
  • Usage access — optional; only to hide the helper when you leave your reading app (1.7).
  • Foreground service (special use) — keeps the helper visible while you read.
  • Run at startup — restores scheduled alerts after the device restarts.
  • Internet — Firebase messaging, update announcements, media you attach, and optional sync.

7. Changes to This Policy
