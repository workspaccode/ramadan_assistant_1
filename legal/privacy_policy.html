Privacy Policy — توبتى (Tawabti)

Last updated: 2026-09-28

توبتى ("Tawabti", "the App") is a prayer times, adhan, Quran, dhikr, and Umrah companion application. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version distributed on Google Play.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Location Data

The App uses your device's precise or approximate location to calculate prayer times, determine Qibla direction, and schedule adhan and iqama notifications.

  • Location is requested only after you grant permission in a system dialog.
  • Location is used only while the app is in use. The App does not have (or need) background location access: when the app is not open, daily prayer alerts are scheduled using your last known location, which is saved on your device.
  • Coordinates are processed on your device to compute prayer times.
  • To display a human-readable city name, the App may send approximate coordinates to the operating system's geocoding service.
  • We do not store your location on any server.

1.2 Notifications & Messaging

With your permission, the App shows local notifications (prayer alerts, iqama reminders, dhikr reminders) and receives messages via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). FCM assigns your device a registration token to deliver messages. Messages may include app updates, announcements, and Ramadan greetings.

1.3 App Settings & Usage Data

The App uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust update announcements and feature configuration. Firebase may automatically collect device and app information (such as device model, OS version, and app version) associated with a resettable installation identifier.

1.4 Data Stored On Your Device Only

Your tasbih counts, wird progress, reminder history, chosen settings, calculation method, and app icon preference are stored locally on your device (local database / preferences). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or use "Reset All Data" in Settings.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — Cloud Messaging and Remote Config. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Shorebird — delivers small Dart code patches (performance fixes, bug fixes) to the App over an encrypted connection. Shorebird does not receive your personal data. See https://shorebird.dev/privacy
  • OpenStreetMap tiles — used only inside the Umrah guide map view. Your location is not sent to the map service. See https://osmfoundation.org/privacy
  • Geocoding services — approximate coordinates may be converted to a city name.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no accounts, no chat, and no user-generated content.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • You may deny or revoke any permission at any time in your device's Settings.
  • You may uninstall the App at any time; all locally stored data is deleted.
  • You may use "Reset All Data" in the App's Settings to erase local data.
  • Because no account exists and no personal data is stored on our servers, there is no additional data to request or delete. For questions, contact us below.

6. Permissions the App Requests

  • Location (while the app is in use) — prayer times & Qibla.
  • Notifications — adhan, iqama, and dhikr reminders.
  • Exact alarms — so adhan rings at the precise prayer time even in Doze mode.
  • Full-screen intent — shows the adhan alert over the lock screen when a prayer time begins.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — keeps adhan audio playing reliably.
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback for tasbih.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised, and material changes will be highlighted in the App or via a notification.
