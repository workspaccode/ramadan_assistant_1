# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Muslims using an Arabic-first mobile companion for prayer times, Quran, dhikr,
reminders and Umrah guidance. English and Urdu remain supported.

## Product Purpose

Make daily worship tools easy to reach and prayer information easy to read.

## Brand Personality

Calm, welcoming and clear. The user requested a comprehensive visual refresh,
with light green or orange and gold, white daytime backgrounds and black dark mode.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded headers, unrelated saturated feature colors, illegible text on
decorative gradients, and layouts that hide labels on small phones.

## Design Principles

- Prayer information and frequently used tools come first.
- Preserve existing workflows while improving hierarchy.
- Use decoration sparingly and consistently.
- Explain missing data and offer a useful next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable Arabic and RTL layouts, comfortable touch targets, responsive wrapping,
and contrasting foregrounds in both themes. Preserve language and theme settings.
